# India Government Employment Source Universe

## Purpose

This is the seed/discovery universe for the Job Operating System's Government Job Discovery layer. It is intentionally designed as a **living source universe**, not a fixed Top-N list. The agent must search these named organisations and then recursively discover their subordinate offices, institutes, laboratories, centres, missions, projects, recruitment pages, PDFs, state bodies, districts and municipal bodies.

## Non-negotiable rule

**The numbers in this document are not a crawl limit.** 250, 1,000, 5,000 or 10,000 are coverage milestones, not maximums. Continue discovering legitimate public employment sources until search dimensions produce diminishing returns. Never fabricate names to reach a numeric threshold.

## Research basis

The named institutional phases were assembled from official government directories and institutional lists, including the National Portal of India directory, CSIR laboratories, ICAR institutions, ICMR institutes, DST autonomous S&T institutions, DBT autonomous institutes, Ministry of Earth Sciences organisations, IIT/NIT recruitment directories and UGC/Ministry of Education university lists. The National Portal explicitly covers ministries, departments, boards/undertakings, district offices, directorates, PSUs/JVs/companies/societies, commissions, missions, statutory/autonomous bodies and institutions. [National Portal](https://www.india.gov.in/directory/web-directory)
